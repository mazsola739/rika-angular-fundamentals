RxJS: Star Wars

The first module of the course was RxJS. The task: make this little Star Wars page work in one component, with the operators of the module. It talks to the free Star Wars API; when that does not answer, a saved copy of its names does.

  • Subject, filter, debounceTime and switchMap: the search waits for {{ minLength }} letters and for you to stop typing, and a new search cancels the old one.
  • forkJoin: the button asks for characters and planets at once and shows one list.
  • combineLatest: the loader watches both requests and covers the page while both run.
